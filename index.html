    MBA 8107 Strategic Management

    战略管理 — 课堂笔记

    Strathmore Business School · MBA

    课程简介 · Purpose of the Course

    来源：课程页 — Purpose of the Course

    This course aims to equip MBA students with a comprehensive understanding of strategic management principles and their applications in contemporary business environments.
    本课程旨在让 MBA 学生全面理解战略管理的原理，及其在当代商业环境中的应用。

    • How organizations formulate, implement, and evaluate strategies to achieve sustainable competitive advantage.
      组织如何制定、实施和评估战略，以获得可持续竞争优势。
    • Analyze complex business situations, develop strategic thinking capabilities, and make effective strategic decisions in dynamic, competitive markets.
      分析复杂的商业情境，培养战略思维能力，在动态竞争的市场中做出有效的战略决策。
    • Emphasis on practical application: case studies, strategic simulations, and real-world examples from various industries.
      强调实践应用：案例研究、战略模拟，以及来自各行业的真实案例。

    Expected Learning Outcomes（预期学习成果）

    Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:（成功完成本课程后，学生将能够：）

    1. Understand and apply fundamental concepts of strategic management to organizational contexts
      理解战略管理的基本概念，并将其应用于组织情境
    2. Conduct comprehensive strategic analysis using various frameworks and tools
      运用各种框架和工具进行全面的战略分析
    3. Formulate effective business-level and corporate-level strategies
      制定有效的业务层战略（business-level）与公司层战略（corporate-level）
    4. Analyze industry dynamics and competitive positioning
      分析行业动态与竞争定位
    5. Evaluate strategic options and make informed strategic decisions
      评估战略选项，做出有依据的战略决策
    6. Understand the role of leadership and organizational culture in strategy implementation
      理解领导力与组织文化在战略实施中的作用
    7. Assess strategic performance and develop corrective actions
      评估战略绩效并制定纠偏措施
    8. Communicate strategic recommendations effectively to various stakeholders
      向各类利益相关者有效地传达战略建议

    Topic 1: Introduction to Strategic Management（战略管理导论）

    来源：课程页 — Topic 1

    Topic outline（本主题大纲）

    • Definition and scope of strategic management
      战略管理的定义与范围
    • The strategic management process: analysis, formulation, implementation, and evaluation
      战略管理流程：分析 → 制定 → 实施 → 评估
    • Levels of strategy: corporate, business, functional
      战略层级：公司层、业务层、职能层
    • Strategic leadership and governance
      战略领导力与公司治理
    • The role of vision, mission, and values in strategy
      愿景、使命与价值观在战略中的作用

    Case（案例）

    Smith, G. (2004), "An evaluation of the corporate culture of Southwest Airlines", Measuring Business Excellence, Vol. 8 No. 4, pp. 26–33. https://doi.org/10.1108/13683040410569389

    This case illustrates how organizational culture and values drive strategic success or failure.
    这个案例说明组织文化与价值观如何决定战略的成败。（西南航空公司企业文化评估）

    Video（视频）：Know your why — 知道你的「为什么」

    Topic 2: Strategic Analysis — External Environment（战略分析：外部环境）

    来源：课程页 — Topic 2

    Topic outline（本主题大纲）

    • Industry analysis using Porter's Five Forces
      用波特五力模型做行业分析
    • PESTEL analysis for macro-environmental scanning
      用 PESTEL 分析扫描宏观环境（政治、经济、社会、技术、环境、法律）
    • Industry life cycle and strategic implications
      行业生命周期及其战略含义
    • Competitive intelligence and environmental scanning
      竞争情报与环境扫描
    • Blue Ocean Strategy concepts
      蓝海战略的概念

    Video（视频）：Simon Sinek — "Start with Why" — 从「为什么」开始

    Case（案例）

    Thomas, V. J., & Maine, E. (2019). Market entry strategies for electric vehicle start-ups in the automotive industry – Lessons from Tesla Motors. Journal of Cleaner Production, 235, 653–663. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jclepro.2019.06.284

    This case demonstrates external analysis in a rapidly changing industry.
    这个案例展示了如何在快速变化的行业中做外部分析。（电动车初创企业的市场进入战略——特斯拉的启示）

    The Strategic Management Process · Step 1 — Strategic Analysis（战略分析）

    • Understand internal strengths and weaknesses
      理解内部的优势与劣势
    • Scan external threats and opportunities
      扫描外部的威胁与机会
    • Tools: SWOT, PESTEL, Porter's Five Forces
      工具：SWOT 分析、PESTEL 分析、波特五力模型
    • Evaluate company resources and capabilities
      评估公司的资源与能力
    • Monitor competitors and market trends
      监测竞争对手与市场趋势

    Step 2 — Strategy Formulation（战略制定）

    实践中的做法（老师讲的流程）

    1. Pre-work: collect the information
      前期准备：收集信息
    2. Board Away Day 1 — Vision: where they want the company to go?
      董事会外出集思会第一天——愿景：他们想让公司走向哪里？
    3. Workshop
      工作坊
    4. Documentation
      文档整理
    5. Board Away Day 2 — 内容没记全，待补

    For a family business（家族企业的战略制定）

    1. Honor the past — 尊重过去
    2. Challenge the current — 挑战现状
    3. Build the future — 构建未来

    要点

    1. Look at the Founder(s)
      先看创始人。
    2. Family Business Charter / constitution: family is bloodline only.
      家族企业章程/宪章：这里的「家族」仅指血亲。
    3. 第 3 点没记上，待补。

    Step 3 — Strategy Implementation（战略实施）

    • Turning plans into action and results
      把计划变成行动和结果
    • Aligning structure, systems, processes
      让组织结构、系统、流程与战略对齐
    • Leadership and culture critical here
      领导力与文化在这一步至关重要
    • Requires communication and coordination
      需要沟通与协调
    • Resource allocation must support goals
      资源配置必须支撑目标
    • Execution is where many strategies fail
      很多战略就是败在执行这一步
